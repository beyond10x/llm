## How the selection was made

`aep plan artifact waves --kind story --status draft` (output below, verbatim) places the four units
in four different waves (1, 2, 4, 6). Taken alone, its wave 1 holds one of them,
`story:text-deltas-are-final`.

This proposal keeps every collision the verb found and sequences around it, as wave 2026-10-16-w08
did:

- Most collisions are on files the coordinator owns in this wave (list below). Units do not write
  them; the coordinator writes them once at integration.
- What remains after those files: capabilities and deltas share
  `crates/llm-responses/tests/live_stream.rs`; capabilities and tool-result share
  `crates/llm-responses/tests/wire_support/mod.rs`; deltas and tool-result share
  `spec/domains/responses.yaml` and `docs/{responses,messages,chat}.md`; harness and deltas share
  `docs/harness-parity.md` (row R36). Those three pairs are in different layers.
- Layer 1's two units share only coordinator-owned files.
