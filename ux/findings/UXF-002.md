At 1440x900 the docs sidebar is a sticky box inside the doc container. When the reader scrolls to the
end of a page, the footer pushes the container up and the sidebar leaves the viewport: gate 2's
"bottom" check finds it clipped on 25 of the 25 pages that have a sidebar, in both themes.

Effect: at the end of a page, where a reader picks the next page, the page list is gone and the
reader scrolls back up.

Frequency 25 of 26 pages (the home page has no sidebar). Cost S (CSS).

Fix direction (one line): a fixed sidebar column of the viewport's height, with the footer starting right of it.

Severity S3: one always-visible element at one viewport, at the end of the page only.

## Closed in R-01

Red: on the R-01 build, `gate2 sidebar:clipped@bottom` failed 50 units on 25 pages at 1440x900, none
waived.

Fix: from 997 px the sidebar column is fixed at the viewport's height with its own scroll, and the
footer starts right of it (`website/src/css/custom.css`).

Green: 0 sidebar failures at the top and the bottom of every page, in both themes; no page gained a
horizontal scroll (`layout.page_scroll` and `layout.overflow` 0).
