Below 997 px Docusaurus hides the navbar's colour-mode toggle and shows it only in the menu that the
hamburger button opens. At 390x844 gate 2 finds no visible theme toggle on any of the 26 pages, in
either theme.

Effect: switching to the dark theme on a phone takes an extra tap to open the menu first, and the
control cannot be seen from the page.

Frequency 26 of 26 pages at 390x844. Cost S (CSS).

Fix direction (one line): keep the toggle in the navbar below 997 px.

Severity S3: one always-visible element at one viewport; the menu still reaches it.

## Closed in R-01

Red: on the R-01 build, `gate2 theme toggle:missing` failed 52 units on 26 pages at 390x844, none
waived, and `l2-findings.spec.ts::UXF-004` found no theme toggle in the navbar. Showing the toggle
alone left it under the search button (`theme toggle:covered` on every page below 997 px).

Fix: below 997 px the toggle stays displayed and the search container joins the navbar row instead
of lying over the right padding (`website/src/css/custom.css`). The menu button, the toggle and the
search button sit side by side at 390, 768 and 1024 px.

Green: 0 theme-toggle failures at 390x844 and at the measured-only 768x1024 and 1024x768. The toggle
cycles system, light, dark, as on a desktop; from "system" in a light scheme the second tap shows the
dark theme, which the asset checks.
