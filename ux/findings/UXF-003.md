axe-core reports `link-in-text-block` (serious, WCAG 1.4.1 Use of Color) on 22 of the 26 pages, in
both themes and at both gating viewports. A link inside a paragraph has no underline, and its colour
contrast with the surrounding text is below 3:1, so it shows only as a colour change. All 272
serious nodes of R-01 are this rule.

Frequency 22 of 26 pages. Cost S (CSS).

Fix direction (one line): underline links in running text, in both themes.

Severity S2: a gate 5 failure on most pages; reading and following the links still works.
