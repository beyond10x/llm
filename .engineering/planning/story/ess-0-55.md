## Outcome

The repository runs on the newest ESS release, 0.55.0: the `ess` CLI its CI installs and its
`ess-conformance` / `ess-primitives` dependencies name tag `0.55.0` instead of `0.52.0`, the suite
is regenerated with it, and `task check` passes against the shared `ess` 0.55.0 on the operator's
machine.

## Why

Operator rule, 2026-10-05: "ALWAYS upgrade to most recent ESS" (`~/beyond10x/AGENTS.md`, "ESS
version"). With `ess` 0.55.0 on `PATH`, `task check` stops at the conformance stage with "suite
drift: regenerate contracts/suite.json with ESS" (gate on 080ffb0d, 2026-10-07).

Projected on `main` f7f4f759 with `ess` 0.55.0 and the gate's own arguments:

- schemas (166 artifacts): identical;
- suite scenarios (691) and coverage: identical, byte for byte;
- `provenance.suite_version`: `ess-conformance/5` becomes `ess-conformance/35`;
- `provenance` gains `scenario_initial_state: empty`.

## Acceptance

- No `0.52.0` ESS pin remains in `checks/conformance/Cargo.toml`, `Cargo.lock` or
  `.github/workflows/gate.yml`; the crates and the CI install name `0.55.0`, and CI checks the
  asset against the release's `SHA256SUMS`.
- `contracts/suite.json` is the 0.55.0 projection; its scenarios and coverage are unchanged (691).
- `ess specify validate`, the conformance check (three runs) and `task check` exit 0 with `ess`
  0.55.0.
- The Gate workflow is green on the pull request head that carries this change into `main`.

## ESS first

None: a toolchain move. The specification does not change; the regenerated difference is the
suite's provenance only.
