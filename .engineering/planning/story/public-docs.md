## Outcome

llm's documentation is one current set: its own site at `https://beyond10x.github.io/llm/` describes 0.1.7, its reference and status pages are generated by a Rust crate and drift-checked, and README.md and AGENTS.md match the site and the code.

## Why

Operator request 2026-10-05: apply the new workspace `docs` skill to llm, and documentation is independent: `https://beyond10x.github.io/llm/` shows the docs, not the "Documentation moved" redirect it serves today. The site predates 0.1.5-0.1.7.

## Acceptance

- `crates/llm-docs` generates `website/docs/reference/crates.md`, `website/data/status.json` (`b10x-status/1`) and `website/docs/status.mdx`; `generate --check` runs in `task check`.
- Every hand-written page describes 0.1.7 behaviour; every command on a page was run; new guides for `b10x-llm-tool-call` and `b10x-llm-blocking`.
- `@beyond10x/docs-system` pinned to round C (`acbc7a39`); the site builds.
- Independent site: `pages.yml` (Documentation validation) and `b10x-docs-site.yml` (Documentation site, Website `project-site.yml`), `baseUrl: /llm/`, `llm-docs provenance`; `b10x.docs.yaml`, the bundle, check and facade workflows removed, as Secrets did (secrets `65b6775`).
- README.md and AGENTS.md updated per the skill.
- After merge: Website drops llm from its lock and redirects its former routes; Atlas lists llm as independently documented and retires its unified surface (as Atlas `0ce1cbc0` did for Secrets); `curl -sL https://beyond10x.github.io/llm/` returns the site.
